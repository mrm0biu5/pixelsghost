The effect

An ordinary camera, an impossible card

  1. The phone is just a phone

    It sits on your home screen. You swipe across and tap Camera, like anyone would. That home screen is a picture, with the real clock and battery live on top.

  2. The card goes up

    Hold the Jack about 25 cm above the screen. A light tap in your hand tells you it has locked on. Nobody else feels it.

  3. The screen lies

    The camera shows the card you chose instead. Move it, tilt it, take it away off the edge of the screen. The fake leaves with it.

Why it fools people

Matched to the room, every two seconds

The fake card is not pasted on. It reads the real card in your hand and copies how the camera sees it.

♦

Ink like the real print

The Jack's two big diamonds and its solid black centre are the reference. The fake's red and black are matched to them in the room's own light.

♣

Grain, focus and blur

Camera grain, out-of-focus softness that grows as the card comes closer, and motion smear at the camera's own exposure time.

♥

A smooth, tight track

The card's own edges firm up every frame. Going off screen, the fake glides out along the card's path instead of vanishing.

♠

Secret controls

Double-tap the flip arrows to reset. Record with sound to Photos. Choose any card as the reveal: the 3♣, or one you photograph.

Try it

Join the beta

PixelsGhost is in private testing for magicians. Testers are invited by email through Apple's TestFlight.

  1. Accept the TestFlight invite on your iPhone.
  2. Install PixelsGhost from the TestFlight app.
  3. Open Settings in the app (hold •••) and read Instructions.
  4. You will need a Jack of Diamonds to hold up.

Works on iPhone with a front camera. Nothing you do in the app leaves your phone.
