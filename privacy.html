PixelsGhost Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

PixelsGhost is a magic trick app: it shows the front camera's picture and replaces a playing card held up to it with a different card on screen.

What the app collects

Nothing. PixelsGhost has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any data to the developer or to anyone else.

How it uses your phone

  • Camera: to show the live picture and find the card. The picture is processed on the phone only and is not stored unless you record.
  • Microphone: only while you record a video in the app, for the video's sound.
  • Photos: to save videos you record, and to let you choose screenshots or card photos. Only the pictures you pick are read.

Screenshots, card photos and settings you choose are kept inside the app on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through TestFlight's feedback option in the app, or the App Store listing.
